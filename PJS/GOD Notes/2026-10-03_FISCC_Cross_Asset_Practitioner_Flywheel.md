## Rule

Do the research before the outreach.

A practitioner conversation should begin with:
1. a concrete hypothesis;
2. evidence;
3. a model / notebook / memo;
4. explicit uncertainties;
5. a short list of high-value questions.

That turns networking into a PBCT multiplier.
