# PJS — HK–SZ Dual-City T0 Base & Proof Economy

## Core thesis

A strong position is earned through evidence that the relevant work can actually be done.

For technical / research roles, PBCT proof may include research, code, experiments, systems, papers, competitions, open-source work and technical interview performance.

For commercial / institutional roles, PACT proof may include company DD, client / stakeholder maps, transaction knowledge, product understanding and real business workflows.

For entrepreneurship / capital formation, PCCT proof may include investor maps, sector theses, funding-stage reasoning, commercialization work and founder / investor relationship graphs.

## Hong Kong × Shenzhen

Shenzhen and Hong Kong can function as one larger operating graph:
- Shenzhen: stable execution, building, research and mainland network.
- Hong Kong: international finance, quant, AI, universities, capital markets, investors and cross-border institutional density.

The strategic objective is to reduce life and commuting friction so that more AlphaTime can be allocated to:
- informal talks;
- applications and interviews;
- research;
- artifacts;
- high-quality relationships.

## PJS implication

The point of normalizing Hong Kong access is not tourism. It is to create repeated exposure to higher-quality people, problems and institutions, then turn that exposure into stronger PACT / PBCT / PCCT artifacts.

**Proof compounds access; access compounds better proof.**
