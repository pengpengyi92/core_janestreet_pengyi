# PJS — PACT Universal Field Intelligence

**Timestamp:** 2026-10-03 20:59 HKT

## Core extension

The same structured thinking used to study Jane Street, quant firms, roles, people and systems should be transferable to **any real business environment**.

PACT provides that transfer layer.

A shopping street, mall, office tower, restaurant cluster, hotel or retail district can be decomposed with the same discipline used in quant due diligence:

**observable node -> legal entity -> business model -> financial structure -> counterparties -> decision makers -> products / needs -> relationship graph**

## Why this belongs in PJS

PJS is not only about memorizing Jane Street facts. It is about learning a high-standard way to reason about organizations, systems and economic activity.

PACT generalizes that reasoning beyond quant:

- retail becomes unit economics + inventory + cash conversion + acquiring;
- commercial property becomes rent roll + occupancy + refinancing + tenant graph;
- restaurants become store economics + franchise / group structure + supply chain;
- hotels become room revenue + F&B + capex + FX + payroll;
- clinics become professional-service economics + equipment + payer / customer mix;
- corporate offices become industry / payroll / treasury / decision-maker maps.

## Transferable skill

The important skill is:

> See a company in the physical world and convert it into a structured model that another professional can inspect, challenge and act on.

This resembles the PJS / JMatrix habit:
- define the object;
- identify the dimensions;
- collect evidence;
- separate observation from inference;
- quantify where possible;
- map people and incentives;
- identify missing information;
- iterate.

## PACT × PBCT

PBCT builds technical artifacts and quantitative systems.

PACT expands the set of real-world problems that can feed those systems.

Together:
- PACT finds and structures the business problem;
- PBCT models, automates or analyzes it;
- conversations and field observations provide feedback;
- the next artifact becomes stronger.

## Causeway Bay example

A district that initially appears to be “retail and restaurants” can be unfolded into:
- anchor retailers;
- landlords;
- office tenants;
- chain operators;
- SMEs;
- suppliers;
- logistics;
- hotels;
- healthcare;
- professional services;
- corporate-banking opportunities.

That is the PJS lesson: **high-quality structured reasoning should travel across industries.**

## Operating principle

Wherever we go, ask whether the environment can be converted into a reusable model, graph, case or artifact.

The world itself can become a training set for PACT × PBCT.
