# PJS — Transit Mastery as Hong Kong Career Infrastructure

**Date:** 2026-10-03

## Core idea

Mastering Hong Kong transit is part of making the Jane Street / Hong Kong path operationally normal.

Priority:
1. East Rail Line — Shenzhen border / New Territories / CUHK / Kowloon / Admiralty.
2. Island Line — Hong Kong Island career and finance corridor.
3. South Island Line — extend spatial understanding south of Admiralty.
4. Learn the rest of the MTR and key bus network until route choice becomes automatic.

## Why this matters

Every reduction in navigation friction returns attention to:
- informal talks;
- interviews;
- field DD;
- research;
- PACT;
- PBCT;
- PCCT;
- artifacts.

Transit itself is also research material. Railway operators, bus companies, infrastructure finance, TOD property models and network-flow data can all become structured cases.

## Four-day field objective

Use 2026-10-04 to 2026-10-07 to broaden the Hong Kong mental map deliberately.

Do not optimize for checking stations off a list. Optimize for understanding:
- where finance / quant / technology / universities cluster;
- how corridors connect;
- what commute friction actually feels like;
- which stations create useful work / meeting / research nodes;
- what business and institutional systems are visible along each route.

Hong Kong becomes more valuable when the city is understood as a graph rather than a set of isolated places.
