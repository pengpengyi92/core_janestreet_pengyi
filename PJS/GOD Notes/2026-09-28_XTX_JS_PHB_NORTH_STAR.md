## Interpretation

XTX and Jane Street represent two different North Stars:

- XTX: maximum organizational density.
- Jane Street: extreme density preserved at much larger scale.

The mechanism is:

**Human Talent × Capital × Compute × Software × Proprietary Data × Market Access × Automation × Institutional Infrastructure.**

The career implication is to prefer institutions where one researcher / engineer / trader can leverage the largest institutional stack, not merely the highest nominal salary.
