# HK Central Hosting + Huanggang Return — Jane Street God Notes

**Date:** 2026-10-01  
**System:** PJS / Jane Street  
**Theme:** HK work-life infrastructure, Central hosting, ETM-AAO routing, Shenzhen↔HK operating loop

## 1. Core operating rule

Hong Kong movement should be **purpose-driven, East-Rail-first, Central/Admiralty-first**.

Default:
- Shenzhen / Futian → Futian Checkpoint → Lok Ma Chau → East Rail → Admiralty.
- Do **not** stop at Mong Kok East, Kowloon Tong, Causeway Bay or other dense urban nodes unless there is a concrete ETM-AAO reason: person, meeting, firm, building, task, recovery, or other explicit objective.
- Passing through a station is not a destination.
- HK should function as a high-frequency work / meeting / DD extension of the Shenzhen base, not as an aimless consumption route.

## 2. Tonight's operating pattern

Preferred one-day loop:

**Shenzhen → Lok Ma Chau → East Rail direct to Admiralty → Central DD / dinner → Wan Chai cross-boundary coach node → Huanggang → Futian / home**

Key implication:
- A hotel is optional rather than required for ordinary HK workdays.
- Central dinner does not force an overnight stay.
- The late-night Huanggang coach route gives a second return channel after the East Rail / Lok Ma Chau window.
- This reduces friction for future Jane Street / Point72 / Jump / Tower / other Central finance meetings that continue into dinner.

For holiday / event days, temporary traffic arrangements can change the exact Wan Chai boarding point. Always verify the same-day cross-boundary terminal before departure.

## 3. Jane Street hosting principle

The objective is not "expensive restaurant". The objective is **conversation quality + low friction + appropriate register**.

For a 1:1 or 2–4 person JS / quant / buy-side dinner, optimize for:
1. Central / Admiralty proximity.
2. Easy post-work arrival from the office.
3. Table spacing and manageable noise.
4. 90–120 minute conversation without pressure to turn tables.
5. Service that does not interrupt technical / career discussion.
6. Smart-casual or business-formal register that feels natural rather than ceremonial.
7. Reliable food and simple ordering.
8. Easy continuation to coffee / drink / MTR / Huanggang return.

The hosting environment should support **PAct: hosting + communication + relationship building**, not distract from it.

## 4. HK Business Dining / Hosting Set

### A. Duddell's — Cantonese Business Dinner Template
**Positioning:** business formal / smart formal  
**Use:** 2–4 people; first serious dinner; senior contact; Cantonese hosting  
**Why it matters:** Central location, formal enough for professional hosting but still usable for conversation.

### B. Beefbar Hong Kong — Quant / Finance Smart-Casual Template
**Positioning:** smart casual → smart formal  
**Use:** trader / quant / engineer / TDOE dinner; first or second informal-professional meeting  
**Why it matters:** serious enough for business but not over-ceremonial; strong fit for finance / technical people after work.

### C. Man Wah — Senior / High-Formality Template
**Positioning:** business formal  
**Use:** more senior guest, established relationship, milestone dinner  
**Why it matters:** Mandarin Oriental / Central context; high reliability and strong hosting signal.  
**Caution:** may be too heavy for an initial casual coffee-chat-style dinner.

### D. Magistracy Dining Room — Deep Conversation Formal Dinner
**Positioning:** business formal  
**Use:** 2–4 people; longer, higher-context conversation  
**Why it matters:** setting can support a full professional occasion rather than a quick meal.

### E. Mott 32 — Social Business / Dinner + Drink
**Positioning:** smart formal / social business  
**Use:** after some familiarity has already been built; dinner plus social extension  
**Why it matters:** stronger social energy; less suitable when maximum conversational quiet is the priority.

### F. Salisterra — Smart-Casual Conversation Template
**Positioning:** smart casual  
**Use:** coffee-chat upgraded into dinner; 1:1 / 2–4 people; relaxed professional talk  
**Why it matters:** lighter register than formal hotel dining while still offering a high-quality setting.

## 6. Field DD checklist

When testing these restaurants in person, record:
- door-to-office travel friction;
- best 2-person and 4-person tables;
- table spacing;
- actual dinner noise level;
- lighting;
- dress profile of guests;
- service interruption frequency;
- ordering complexity / shareability;
- realistic spend per person;
- whether 90–120 min conversation feels natural;
- whether coffee / drink extension nearby is easy;
- late-night MTR / Wan Chai → Huanggang return friction.

A restaurant is only promoted to **T0 JS Hosting Node** after real-world DD.
