# Futian T0 Base — Long-Run Support for the Jane Street Path

**Date:** 2026-10-01

## Core idea

The Jane Street Hong Kong path can be supported by a stable **central Futian / PAFC corridor Base** in Shenzhen.

The value of the Base is not distance alone.

It provides:
- strong daily living;
- recovery;
- finance / quant / tech optionality;
- low-friction border access;
- repeatable East Rail launch into Admiralty / Central;
- enough stability to sustain long preparation and career compounding.

## Strategic model

**Shenzhen / Futian**
= Base / Build / Recover / Accumulate

**Hong Kong / Central**
= Jane Street / Career / Global Capital / High-quality People

The bridge is the commute system.

## Why this matters

A high-quality Base helps:
- prepare better;
- sleep better;
- arrive with more energy;
- preserve optionality;
- continue building outside work;
- accumulate capital over years.

The goal is not to maximize time physically spent in Hong Kong.

The goal is to maximize the quality of work and relationships when there.

## Long-run rule

> **Base where life works best; commute purposefully into the highest-quality career node.**
