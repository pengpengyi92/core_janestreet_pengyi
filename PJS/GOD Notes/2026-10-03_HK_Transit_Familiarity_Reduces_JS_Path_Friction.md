# PJS — Hong Kong Transit Familiarity Reduces JS-Path Friction

**Date:** 2026-10-03

## Field insight

Hong Kong MTR and bus usage feels operationally familiar and, subjectively, reminiscent of the UK transport experience.

That matters for the Jane Street / Hong Kong path because migration friction is not only about visas, housing or salary. It is also about whether daily movement feels intuitive enough that work, study, interviews and social / professional activity can happen without repeatedly consuming attention.

## PJS implication

A familiar transport environment lowers the cost of:
- commuting to Central / Admiralty / Wan Chai;
- reaching Chater House and other finance nodes;
- attending informal talks and interviews;
- visiting universities and research groups;
- returning to Shenzhen through established cross-border routes.

The easier Hong Kong becomes operationally, the more AlphaTime can be allocated to PBCT, PACT, interviews and artifacts rather than navigation overhead.

## Principle

**Infrastructure familiarity is part of career infrastructure.**

Hong Kong should therefore be evaluated not only as a job location, but as a place where the daily operating layer already feels increasingly natural and reusable.
