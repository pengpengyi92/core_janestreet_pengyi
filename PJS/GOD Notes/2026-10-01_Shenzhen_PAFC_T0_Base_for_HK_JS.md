# Shenzhen PAFC / Futian T0 Base for the HK Jane Street Path

**Date:** 2026-10-01

## Thesis

A Jane Street Hong Kong path does not require Hong Kong to become the default home base immediately.

A strong alternative operating model is:

**PAFC / central Futian Base**
→ Futian Checkpoint
→ Lok Ma Chau
→ East Rail
→ Admiralty / Central
→ Jane Street / quant / finance mission
→ Hong Kong Island return gateway
→ Shenzhen Base.

## Why this matters

A comfortable Shenzhen base can increase, rather than reduce, Hong Kong career intensity if it:
- protects sleep and recovery;
- lowers living friction;
- makes early launch repeatable;
- provides strong building infrastructure;
- keeps Shenzhen quant / tech / fund optionality alive;
- permits selective, purpose-driven HK travel.

## PAFC / Futian advantages for PJS

- familiarity and low cognitive overhead;
- finance / institutional environment;
- business hosting infrastructure;
- proximity to Futian Checkpoint;
- strong connectivity across Shenzhen;
- ability to combine Shenzhen PBCT opportunities with Hong Kong Jane Street / Central opportunities;
- suitable recovery base after intense HK meetings or DD.

## Career principle

Do not confuse proximity with commitment.

Living directly beside a target firm is not automatically optimal. The better base is the one that maximizes:
**preparation + health + repeated access + building + relationship quality.**

## Working-day discipline

If the goal is to simulate a real Jane Street / Central workday:
- wake early;
- leave like a normal employee;
- use the border / East Rail route as infrastructure;
- arrive before the intended work block;
- work from the city rather than treating the trip as sightseeing.

## Anti-aftershock

A purposeless HK branch must not damage the next day.

> **Jane Street preparation is a multi-day system. Protect tomorrow during today.**

PAFC / Futian is therefore classified as a **supporting T0 Base node for the PJS Hong Kong strategy**.
