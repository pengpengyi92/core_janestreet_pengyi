# PJS — Dual Artifact Flywheel: PACT × PBCT

**Date:** 2026-10-03

## Core thesis

The long-run capability stack should accumulate two different but complementary classes of proof.

### PACT artifacts

PACT is built from:
- resources;
- connections;
- information;
- transaction knowledge;
- product knowledge;
- organizational and commercial workflows.

Its artifacts should show that these can be understood and mobilized:
- company / industry DD;
- relationship maps;
- stakeholder graphs;
- transaction / product playbooks;
- corporate-banking cases;
- client / supplier / tenant maps;
- informal-talk outputs;
- opportunity and pipeline maps.

### PBCT artifacts

PBCT is the hard technical proof layer:
- frontier research understanding;
- machine learning;
- quantitative research;
- coding;
- systems;
- experimentation;
- reproducibility.

Its artifacts should be externally inspectable:
- code;
- papers;
- benchmarks;
- notebooks;
- reproductions;
- open-source PRs;
- competition results;
- production systems;
- technical interview evidence.

## PJS interpretation

A strong profile should not be described only by adjectives such as “technical,” “commercial,” or “well connected.”

It should be demonstrated by two evidence stacks.

**PACT shows the ability to move through real institutions, people, resources, information and transactions.**

**PBCT shows the ability to solve hard technical and research problems with verifiable evidence.**

The highest-value state is the interaction between them:

**PACT finds higher-value problems, people and information  
→ PBCT converts them into rigorous artifacts  
→ strong artifacts improve access and conversations  
→ better access generates better problems and information  
→ repeat.**

## Operating rule

Continuously ask:

- What new PACT artifact was created?
- What new PBCT artifact was created?
- Did one improve the other?
- Can an external interviewer, practitioner or collaborator inspect the proof?

This dual-artifact flywheel should compound over years.
