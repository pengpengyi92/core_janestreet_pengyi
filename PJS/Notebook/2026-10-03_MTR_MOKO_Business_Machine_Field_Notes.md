# PJS Notebook — MTR × MOKO × Business-Machine Field Notes

**Date:** 2026-10-03

## Field insight

Transport, malls and quant firms can all be analyzed using a common systems habit:

**inputs → processes → costs → outputs → incentives → constraints → feedback.**

MTR:
- passenger and commercial revenue;
- staff / maintenance / electricity / operations;
- depreciation and financing;
- stations / property / TOD;
- large-scale operating and governance systems.

MOKO / Mong Kok East:
- station-linked footfall;
- mall tenants;
- restaurants;
- retail;
- transport interchange;
- surrounding commercial ecosystem.

Jane Street:
- people;
- capital;
- data;
- models;
- technology;
- risk systems;
- collaboration and decision loops.

The domains differ, but the analytical habit is reusable: reconstruct the system beneath the visible surface.
